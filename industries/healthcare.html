Privacy & Compliance

Built around what a hospital can and can't say in public.

A hotel can reply to a review about a noisy room. A hospital replying to a review has to think about patient privacy first, every time. That discipline is built into how we work, not bolted on afterward.

✓

No patient details in public replies

Responses never confirm a patient relationship, diagnosis, treatment, or visit detail, even when a reviewer volunteers that information themselves.

✓

Clinical concerns get escalated, not answered

Any review touching a care or safety concern is routed straight to your designated staff, rather than receiving a generic public reply.

✓

Approval workflows you control

You decide whether responses post within agreed guidelines automatically, or require your team's sign-off before anything goes live.

A Note On How We Frame This

We describe our workflows as privacy-aware and built with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) in mind, the law that actually governs patient data handling for hospitals operating in India.

We're not a law firm or a compliance auditor, and we don't issue compliance certifications. We recommend every hospital have its own compliance team review our response protocols and sign off on them before launch, and we're glad to work directly with yours to do that.

Questions

Common healthcare questions.

Does ReviewLogik replace our hospital's own patient relations or quality team?

No, ReviewLogik surfaces sentiment, themes, and escalations for your patient relations and quality teams to act on. Anything touching a clinical or safety concern routes straight to your staff rather than receiving a public reply. It's intelligence for your team, not a replacement for it.

Which platforms does ReviewLogik monitor for hospitals?

Google, Practo, JustDial, and Facebook today, alongside patient satisfaction survey responses.

Is the Kerala Hospital Reputation Index a ranking?

No, it is a certification, not a competitive ranking. Every hospital that meets the standard after a ReviewLogik audit is recognized; no hospital is scored publicly without completing an audit first.
