Questions

Have questions? We've got answers.

What is ReviewLogik?

ReviewLogik is an Experience Intelligence Platform that helps hospitality and healthcare organizations monitor, analyze, and respond to guest and patient feedback, turning reviews and sentiment into intelligence executives can act on.

Which industries does ReviewLogik serve?

Two, today: Hospitality (hotels, resorts, restaurants, and spas) and Healthcare (hospitals and clinics), on one shared platform.

What is the Kerala Hospital Reputation Index?

ReviewLogik's annual, audit-based certification recognizing hospitals across Kerala for patient experience excellence, modeled on the same principle as TripAdvisor's Certificate of Excellence.

How is ReviewLogik different from a review-monitoring tool?

Rather than only monitoring and responding to reviews, ReviewLogik turns that feedback into executive-level intelligence: sentiment trends, department-level insight, and recommended actions, delivered through executive dashboards.
