Our Differentiator

Privacy comes first. Always.

Every response is written against a protocol designed specifically for patient confidentiality and clinical liability -- reviewed before it ever goes public, and routed to your team the moment a reply risks saying too much. Built around India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) -- not a framework built for another country's law.

What We Never Do
  • Confirm or imply a patient's presence, diagnosis, or treatment in a public reply
  • Argue a clinical or billing dispute in the comments -- it routes to your team instead
  • Post a response without a defined escalation path for anything serious
