Questions

About the Index.

What is the Kerala Hospital Reputation Index?

An annual, audit-based certification from ReviewLogik that recognizes hospitals across Kerala for patient experience excellence, modeled on the same principle as TripAdvisor's Certificate of Excellence.

Is it a ranking of hospitals?

No. It is a certification, not a competitive ranking: every hospital that meets the standard is recognized, and no hospital is scored publicly without completing a ReviewLogik audit first.

How is the Patient Experience Score (PES) calculated?

PES is a weighted 0-100 composite: 25% rating, 20% sentiment, 10% each for response rate, response time, satisfaction trend, review growth, and service recovery, and 5% complaint index. Full methodology is published with every Index edition.

How does a hospital join the next edition of the Index?

Four steps: nominate the hospital, complete a free ReviewLogik audit, go through certification, and publication in the Index.
